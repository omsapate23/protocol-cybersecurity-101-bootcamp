LEVEL 4: THE SILENT POSTER (8-10 minutes, easy)

The briefing is recovered, but one attachment is still unexplained.
An organiser sent poster.png and said: "The picture is only part of the file."
Looking at it normally shows a simple event poster. Find the concealed message
inside the file and recover one PROTOCOL{...} flag.

1. Identify the file's real format.
2. Look for readable text inside it.
3. Use a filter if the output is noisy.

Useful tools: file, strings, grep
Need help? Read HINTS.txt one hint at a time.
Check your flag from this directory: python3 ../check.py
Select level 4. This level can also be played on its own.

Use the original poster.png file from the ZIP or repository.
A screenshot or re-saved image may remove the concealed message.
